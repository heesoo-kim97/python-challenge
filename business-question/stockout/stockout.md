# Identify Products at Risk of Stockout

Business Scenario:
A beverage distributor wants to identify products that may run out of inventory soon. You are given a dataset with:
- SKU
- Current_Inventory
- Avg_Daily_Sales
- Lead_Time_Days

Business Question: Write Python code using pandas to calculate the Days of Supply for each SKU and identify products where inventory will run out before the next shipment arrives.
